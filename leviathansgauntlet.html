01 // Why

Why I made it

Leviathan's Gauntlet was a collaborative project with 29 people building one game in eight weeks.

At that size the hardest problem isn't the game, it's keeping everyone's work landing in one stable project. That's why I took on the tech lead role.

02 // How

How I built it

I managed the Git repository and technical integration for the whole team, resolving merge conflicts as work came in.

Alongside that I supported gameplay development across the team, helping people get their features into the build.
