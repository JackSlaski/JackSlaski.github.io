01 // Why

Why I made it

Arms & Assets comes from the three years I spent running Garry's Mod roleplay servers, where the freedom and the player economy were at the heart of the game.

I wanted to build those systems properly in Unreal Engine 5: items that feel unique, an inventory you can trust, and an economy players can trade in.

Where it's at

I started Arms & Assets before my MSc, developed it for the Advanced Game Design and Research module, where it got 74%, and kept building it after.

It's on hold for now. Work so far covers weapon systems, randomly generated item stats, inventories, item identification, upgrading, gem slots, player analytics, admin interfaces, and persistent ownership data.

02 // How

How I built it

Items are generated, not hand-made. Weapons roll random stats, carry their own identity, and can be upgraded and socketed with gems.

Inventory, ownership, and trading all work from the same item data, so an item keeps its identity wherever it goes.

I added analytics and admin tools early, the same way I balanced my Garry's Mod servers from live player data.
