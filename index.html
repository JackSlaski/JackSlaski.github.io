I’ve been making games since 2020, when I started running Garry’s Mod servers and taught myself Lua to build their systems.

Since then I’ve finished a First Class BSc in Computer Games Development and an MSc in Computer Games Design with Distinction, both at the University of Staffordshire. I like systems players can poke at: crafting, inventories, economies, and anything with numbers underneath.

On team projects I usually end up as tech lead or producer. I look after Git and integration, and build the core gameplay everyone else plugs into.

  1. 2020–2023

  2. 2022–2025
