01 // Why

Why I made it

This was my first time building UI in Unreal, or anywhere really, and it's where I realised how much I enjoy it.

It was a learning project: I wanted to build a complete RPG interface, not just a few screens, and learn UMG properly along the way.

02 // How

How I built it

I built every screen in UMG on top of an RPG framework, from the HUD to the shop, inventory, and settings.

I tried to keep every widget modular and technically sound so it could be reused and got the most out of the engine.
