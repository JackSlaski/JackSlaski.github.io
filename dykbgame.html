Do You Know Ball?

A mobile football trivia game. Answer each question by shooting a penalty at the right answer panel.

01 // Why

Why I made it

Most football quiz games are just multiple choice on a screen.

I wanted the answer itself to be the skill: you shoot a penalty at the right answer, so knowing football and timing your shot both matter.

02 // How

How I built it

I planned it in a game design document and a technical design document, then built it in UE5 for phones.

The UI builds itself from the content, so adding questions or teams doesn't mean building new screens.

I added the systems a live mobile game needs: daily rewards, analytics to drive updates, a leaderboard, and placeholder ad and social hooks.

04 // Modes

Three ways to play

01

Quick play

Pick a national team and face an AI opponent. Question difficulty follows the team's ranking: easy, medium, or hard.

02

Versus

Two players share one device, taking turns to answer questions and take penalties.

03

Endless

Keep taking penalties until you get one wrong, and see how long a streak you can build.
