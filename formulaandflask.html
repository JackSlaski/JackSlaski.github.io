Formula and Flask

A first-person potion factory. Experiment with ingredients, sell what you brew, then automate the whole production line.

01 // Why

Why I made it

I wanted a factory game where the fun starts with experimenting and turns into optimising.

You work out a good potion by hand, then build a production line that makes it for you.

02 // How

How I built it

Ingredient combinations are generated fresh each session, so recipes have to be discovered rather than looked up.

Potion quality is judged with fuzzy logic, so you get graded results rather than a simple right or wrong.

Cauldrons and machines are placeable entities with their own settings, which is what turns brewing into automation.

Built with
  • UE5
  • Blueprints
  • Fuzzy logic
  • Procedural generation

04 // Features

What's in it

  • Potion combinations generated fresh each session
  • Fuzzy logic for judging potion quality
  • Five recipes to discover and optimise
  • Buy, sell, and production machines you place in the world
  • Automated brewing with configurable cauldrons
  • A build mode for laying out the factory
  • Money and progression tied to potion sales
  • Recipes that unlock as you level up
  • First-person factory management
