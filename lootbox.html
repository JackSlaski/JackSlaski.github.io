Loot box ethics

A literature review and research portfolio on loot boxes, gambling, and their effect on younger and vulnerable players.

01 // Why

Why I made it

Loot boxes sit very close to gambling, and younger and vulnerable players are the most exposed to them.

I wanted to understand how ethical they really are, and what that means for how games should be designed.

02 // How

How I built it

The literature review covered academic and industry research on gambling-like mechanics, psychological risk, player vulnerability, and regulation.

The research portfolio then analysed modern games with loot box mechanics: how they're presented, priced, and designed to keep players engaged, from reward randomness and rarity to how open the odds are.

Built with
  • Literature review
  • Content analysis
