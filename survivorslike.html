Survivors Like

A fast arena shooter built around game feel, with bouncing projectiles, area attacks, and layered feedback.

01 // Why

Why I made it

It was built for a module on game feel, so the point was to take simple combat and make it feel great to play.

02 // How

How I built it

I layered feedback on every action: Niagara effects, sound, damage numbers, knockback, death effects, and animated cooldowns.

Movement got the same treatment. The character leans into its momentum, and projectiles bounce with physics.

Four area attacks, Shockwave, Raise Hell, Enemy Freeze, and Explosive Mine, give you ways out as the waves get harder.
