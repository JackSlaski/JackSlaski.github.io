    Formula and Flask

    Information

    • Team Size: Solo Project
    • Role: Game Prototype Development
    • Time Frame: 8 Weeks
    • Engine: UE5
    • Grade Achieved: 80%

    Description

    Formula and Flask is a first-person potion factory prototype developed in Unreal Engine 5. Players experiment with ingredient combinations to create potions of varying quality, which can then be sold to earn money and unlock new production opportunities. As the game progresses, manual potion creation can be expanded into a semi-automated production line through the placement of cauldrons and production entities, shifting gameplay from experimentation to optimisation and efficiency.

    Features

    • Procedural Potion Combinations Generated Each Session
    • Fuzzy Logic System For Evaluating Potion Quality
    • Five Unique Potion Recipes To Discover And Optimise
    • Buy, Sell, And Production Entities That Can Be Placed In The World
    • Automated Potion Production Through Configurable Cauldrons
    • Build Mode Allowing Production Layouts To Be Customised
    • Money And Progression Systems Tied To Potion Sales
    • Unlockable Potion Recipes Through Player Levelling
    • First-Person Factory Management Gameplay
